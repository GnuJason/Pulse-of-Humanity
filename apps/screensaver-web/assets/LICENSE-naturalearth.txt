Natural Earth — Public Domain
=============================

The world-110m.json file is a TopoJSON distillation of Natural Earth's 110m
"Admin 0 — Countries" layer, distributed via the world-atlas npm package
(https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas).

Natural Earth is in the public domain. You may use the map and data in any
manner, including modifying the content and design, electronic dissemination,
and offset printing. Attribution is appreciated but not required:

    Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.

See: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
